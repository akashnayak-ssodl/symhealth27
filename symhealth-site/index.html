About SYMHEALTH 2026

On behalf of Symbiosis International (Deemed University), a two-day national conference on Embracing Smart Healthcare: Predictive, Precision and Personalized Health Systems, a pathway to achieving SDGs for healthier future generations, is being hosted at Symbiosis International University, Lavale, Pune.

This annual event, with a legacy of 25 years, attracts over 1,400 delegates from all verticals of healthcare: doctors, medico-legal experts, insurance and IT professionals, NGOs, hospital administrators and clinical research professionals from India and abroad. It offers stalwart speakers, contemporary topics, paper presentation, master classes and networking.

What the conference covers

Three ideas run through both days.

Predictive

Data and AI that spot health risks early and guide decisions before illness sets in.

Precision

Care shaped by genetics, diagnostics and evidence for each patient group.

Personalized

Health systems built around the individual, from hospital to home.
